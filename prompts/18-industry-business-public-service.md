# Industry, Business and Public-Service Video Prompts

Original MiniMax H3 prompts for manufacturing, logistics, workplace communication, and public information. Validate procedures, legal requirements, accessibility, and factual claims with the responsible organization.

## IND-001 Small-Batch Assembly Process

**Status:** Concept — not independently tested

**Use it for:** A manufacturing story where tool use, part count, and quality checks remain mechanically credible.

**Mode:** Workstation images + product parts sheet + approved process sequence + worker reference  \
**Format:** 16:9, target 15 seconds  \
**Reference map:** Images 1–3 = exact workstation and tools; Image 4 = approved part set; Text 1 = verified step order; Image 5 = consenting adult worker and PPE.

```text
Create a clean process film showing final assembly of one fictional aluminum desk fan at a small workshop. Preserve the workstation, jig, torque driver, four fasteners, fan housing, grille, cable, worker identity, and PPE from the references.

Show four verified steps: seat the motor housing in the jig; route the cable through the existing channel; position the grille using its alignment notch; tighten four fasteners in the approved cross pattern. Use close views for contact points and a stable overhead view for the pattern. The driver stops after each fastener; do not show a torque value unless added later from verified production data. End with the unpowered fan on the inspection mat while the worker checks grille clearance by hand.

Sound intent: workshop ambience, part contact, four driver stops, no voiceover. Keep part count, tool geometry, cable route, PPE, and machine state continuous. Avoid spinning blades, missing guards, invented efficiency claims, logos, readable serial numbers, extra hands, sparks, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Process order, fastener count, tool contact, PPE, cable routing, and no fabricated metrics.

## IND-002 Cold-Chain Parcel Journey

**Status:** Concept — not independently tested

**Use it for:** A logistics explainer showing custody and temperature-monitoring touchpoints without inventing compliance.

**Mode:** Package images + facility references + approved route diagram + scanner UI states  \
**Format:** 16:9, target 15 seconds  \
**Reference map:** Images 1–2 = exact fictional parcel and seal; Images 3–5 = facilities; Image 6 = verified route; Images 7–8 = approved scanner states.

```text
Create a concise chain-of-custody journey for one fictional insulated parcel containing non-medical test material. Keep the same parcel ID represented only by a simple colored shape; add any real code later in editing. Preserve box geometry, blue seal, reusable cold pack count, scanner, and facility layouts from the references.

Beat 1: packing bench, two cold packs placed around one sealed sample box and lid closed. Beat 2: parcel crosses the facility scan point; show the approved green confirmation state from Image 7. Beat 3: controlled van interior, parcel secured on the same shelf without opening. Beat 4: receiving bench, intact blue seal checked and approved state from Image 8 shown. End before unpacking.

Sound intent: cardboard, scanner tone, van ambience, no narration. Keep parcel appearance, seal, custody order, orientation, and object count exact. Do not display invented temperatures, timestamps, addresses, patient data, guarantees, medical claims, company logos, generated text, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Custody order, parcel persistence, seal state, privacy, UI fidelity, and claim boundaries.

## IND-003 Inclusive Emergency-Exit Reminder

**Status:** Concept — not independently tested

**Use it for:** A public-information clip that communicates one venue's reviewed evacuation route with accessible visual pacing.

**Mode:** Verified floor plan + location images + approved route + diverse consenting cast  \
**Format:** 9:16, target 15 seconds  \
**Reference map:** Image 1 = current floor plan; Images 2–4 = exact corridor and exit; Text 1 = safety-officer-approved route; Images 5–7 = adult cast identity and mobility aids.

```text
Create a calm vertical emergency-exit reminder for a fictional community arts center, based only on the approved route. Start in the main studio, move through the east corridor, pass the accessible refuge point without blocking it, and finish outside at the documented assembly area. Preserve doors, ramps, handrails, lighting, and exit hardware exactly.

Show three adults moving at a controlled pace: one walking, one using a manual wheelchair, and one using a cane. They travel together without anyone being carried or used as a dramatic obstacle. Camera stays ahead at chest height, shows thresholds and turning space, and never reverses route direction. End on a stable wide shot of the assembly area with empty space for approved instructions and captions added in post.

Sound intent: clear room tone, one neutral alarm sample at safe level, no panic, speech, or music. Avoid smoke, fire, crowd crush, blocked exits, invented signage, unverified timing claims, accessibility stereotypes, generated text, logos, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Approved geography, accessibility, door direction, calm pacing, audio level, and local safety review.

## IND-004 Bilingual Service-Desk Welcome

**Status:** Concept — not independently tested

**Use it for:** A multilingual customer-service orientation with accurate speaker turns, respectful tone, and no private information.

**Mode:** Staff and visitor references + service-desk location + authorized voices  \
**Format:** 16:9, target 12 seconds  \
**Reference map:** Image 1 = adult staff identity and uniform; Image 2 = adult visitor identity; Image 3 = exact desk and queue layout; Audios 1–2 = each person's consented voice identity.

```text
Create a welcoming service-desk vignette in a fictional public library. Preserve the staff member on screen right, visitor on screen left, modest uniforms, desk height, hearing-loop symbol placeholder area, and open path from the references. No real patron records or readable screens.

0–4 seconds: medium two-shot. The staff member makes eye contact and says in English, “Welcome. How can I help today?” The visitor listens with no lip movement. 4–8 seconds: the visitor answers in Mandarin, “我想办理一张借阅证。” The staff member listens and nods once. 8–12 seconds: the staff member gestures toward a blank paper form and replies in Mandarin, “好的，我来为您说明步骤。” End before personal details are requested.

Keep exact speaker-to-voice ownership, natural pronunciation, calm facial motion, respectful personal distance, eyelines, and screen direction. Sound intent: intelligible dialogue with quiet library ambience, no music. Avoid subtitles baked into the image, voice swapping, private data, invented eligibility claims, logos, background face duplication, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Bilingual accuracy, lip sync, speaker attribution, privacy, accessibility, and service-policy claims.
