# Brand and Advertising Video Prompts

Original MiniMax H3 prompts for brand films, campaign launches, product storytelling, and channel adaptation.

## BRD-001 Midnight Observatory Tea Launch

**Use it for:** A premium launch film that makes a new bottled tea feel precise, calm, and contemporary.

**Mode:** Product image + location image + optional sound reference  
**Format:** 16:9, target 12–15 seconds  
**Reference map:** Image 1 = exact bottle geometry and label; Image 2 = mountaintop observatory architecture and color mood; Audio 1 = tempo and restraint only.

```text
Create a refined launch film for the fictional tea brand “NORTH WINDOW”. The story takes place before dawn inside a quiet mountaintop observatory. Preserve the bottle from Image 1 exactly: tall smoked-glass silhouette, pale stone label, dark green cap, no invented claims. Use Image 2 only for the circular room, telescope structure, and blue-hour palette.

Beat 1: a narrow band of starlight travels across the observatory table and reveals the bottle silhouette; camera makes a slow, level push-in. Beat 2: condensation gathers naturally, one droplet follows the bottle curve, while the telescope roof opens and cool sky light enters. Beat 3: the bottle remains still as the horizon changes from indigo to a restrained amber line; settle on a clean hero frame with safe negative space above.

Lighting is physically motivated, with glass refraction, soft metal reflections, and realistic condensation. Sound intent: quiet roof mechanism, distant wind, one low glass tone, no voiceover. Preserve logo spelling and label placement. Avoid floating ingredients, liquid explosions, label mutation, aggressive lens flares, extra text, other brands, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Bottle shape, label fidelity, reflection continuity, condensation path, and usable copy space.

## BRD-002 Community Cinema Opening Night

**Use it for:** A warm local-business campaign film with real people and a clear event feeling.

**Mode:** Location image + cast reference image + optional ambient audio  
**Format:** 9:16, target 10–12 seconds  
**Reference map:** Image 1 = cinema facade; Image 2 = fictional staff identity and wardrobe; Audio 1 = crowd ambience only.

```text
Produce a vertical opening-night campaign for an independent neighborhood cinema called “LANTERN SCREEN”. Preserve the brick facade, turquoise ticket window, and marquee proportions from Image 1. Preserve the two adult staff members from Image 2: same faces, hair, cream shirts, and rust-red aprons.

Opening beat: close handheld view of one staff member placing the final blank poster card inside the ticket window; no readable generated event copy. Middle beat: camera steps backward through the doorway as warm lights turn on in sequence and the second staff member opens the curtain, revealing a small audience entering with natural smiles. Final beat: exterior medium-wide shot; rain has just stopped, pavement reflections glow, both staff stand in the doorway and exchange a relieved look while people pass into the cinema.

Keep the feeling candid, inclusive, and local rather than luxury advertising. Use believable phone-camera micro-movement without focus hunting. Sound intent: ticket stub tear, door bell, low crowd murmur, rain drip. Avoid duplicated guests, face drift, unreadable marquee text, fake reviews, celebrity likeness, third-party posters, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Identity consistency, crowd duplication, facade geometry, platform-safe framing, and absence of fabricated claims.

## BRD-003 One Campaign, Three Aspect Ratios

**Use it for:** Adapting a single outdoor campaign into landscape, feed, and vertical compositions without hard cropping.

**Mode:** Product image + visual system image + optional motion reference  
**Format:** 16:9, 4:5, and 9:16 variants  
**Reference map:** Image 1 = exact compact speaker; Image 2 = color/material system; Video 1 = pace of camera movement only.

```text
Create three native compositions for the fictional portable speaker “FIELD NOTE”, each telling the same six-second idea: a picnic begins quietly, the speaker powers on, and the scene gains gentle human energy. Preserve the speaker from Image 1 exactly—rounded rectangular body, woven moss fabric, centered copper dial. Use Image 2 only for moss, clay, cream, and copper color relationships.

16:9: speaker foreground on a linen picnic cloth, friends enter the wide background from both sides, slow lateral slide. 4:5: speaker lower center, hands place cups around it, camera tilts from product to shared smiles. 9:16: speaker centered in the safe zone, one hand turns the dial, camera rises to reveal a tree canopy and friends leaning into frame.

Keep identical time of day, product finish, wardrobe palette, and emotional beat across variants. Sound intent: dial click, soft fabric movement, light percussion reference only. Do not stretch or crop the speaker, invent buttons, cover it with hands, change the group identity, add visible brand names beyond the supplied label, or add text.
```

**Review:** Native composition per ratio, product invariants, comparable story beat, and safe areas.
