# Transitions, Comedy and Social Video Prompts

Original MiniMax H3 prompts for match cuts, loops, deadpan visual jokes, social hooks, and safe viral-format experimentation.

## SOC-001 Three Rooms, One Rolling Orange

**Status:** Concept — not independently tested

**Use it for:** A seamless match-cut transition demonstrating location continuity through one object.

**Mode:** Three room images + object reference + camera-path sketch  
**Format:** 9:16, target 9 seconds  
**Reference map:** Images 1–3 = kitchen, studio, and greenhouse; Image 4 = exact orange; Image 5 = screen direction.

```text
Create an original three-location match-cut film connected by one rolling orange. Preserve each room's geometry from Images 1–3 and the same natural orange from Image 4. Maintain left-to-right screen direction and identical object scale at every transition.

0–3s kitchen: orange rolls across a pale counter, passes behind a ceramic bowl, and exits frame-right. 3–6s studio: it emerges from behind a matching circular speaker at frame-left, rolls across a gray floor, and passes behind a round stool. 6–9s greenhouse: it emerges from behind a terracotta pot, rolls along a wooden bench, slows, and stops in a small patch of sun. Camera tracks laterally at constant height; each occlusion hides the cut completely.

Sound intent: surface-specific rolling sound in each room, one soft transition click, no music required. Avoid changing fruit size or color, reverse direction, teleportation, extra oranges, moving architecture, branded objects, text, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Object identity, screen direction, occlusion alignment, surface sound, and clean hidden cuts.

## SOC-002 Office Plant Quietly Takes Over

**Status:** Concept — not independently tested

**Use it for:** A deadpan workplace comedy with progressive environmental change.

**Mode:** Office image + plant reference + fictional employee reference  
**Format:** 4:5, target 12 seconds  
**Reference map:** Image 1 = office geometry; Image 2 = pothos species and pot; Image 3 = adult employee identity.

```text
Create a dry, understated office comedy. Preserve the small office from Image 1, the single pothos in a yellow pot from Image 2, and the adult employee from Image 3 wearing the same blue shirt and glasses.

Shot 1: employee waters the modest plant, sets the cup down, and returns to typing. Shot 2, same locked composition later: one vine has reached the keyboard; employee calmly moves it aside without reacting. Shot 3, same composition later: vines now cover the chair back and frame the monitor, but do not grow through solid objects; employee trims one leaf, looks at the camera for half a second, and continues working. End as a new vine gently presses the elevator button outside the glass wall.

Use ordinary fluorescent daylight, progressive continuity, and deadpan timing. Sound intent: keyboard, water, one leaf snip, elevator ding. Avoid instant explosive growth, horror tone, trapping the employee, changing office layout, extra plants, copied sitcom cues, text, logos, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Growth progression, plant physics, locked framing, performer continuity, and safe comic tone.

## SOC-003 Alien at the Self-Service Laundry

**Status:** Concept — not independently tested

**Use it for:** An original social comedy built on one mundane misunderstanding.

**Mode:** Creature design + laundromat image + appliance interaction sketch  
**Format:** 9:16, target 10 seconds  
**Reference map:** Image 1 = original friendly creature; Image 2 = laundromat geometry; Image 3 = washer controls and action order.

```text
Create a vertical deadpan comedy in the quiet self-service laundry shown in Image 2. Preserve the original small creature from Image 1: mint-green skin, three eyes, short navy raincoat, two hands, compact proportions. It is friendly and fictional. Follow the washer interaction in Image 3 without changing controls.

Opening: creature studies a round washer door and carefully places one tiny striped sock inside. It closes the door, inserts one plain token, and presses the single approved button. When the drum begins turning, all three eyes track the sock in perfect circular synchronization. The creature slowly takes out a notebook, draws one simple spiral with no readable words, then notices the matching sock already worn on one foot and looks directly at camera in silent embarrassment.

Sound intent: token drop, button beep, washer tumble, one coat rustle. Keep the humor quiet and physical. Avoid scary alien design, extra limbs, washer misuse, body entering the machine, generated labels, known character resemblance, brand logos, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Creature design lock, appliance causality, eye tracking, sock continuity, and safe family-friendly payoff.
