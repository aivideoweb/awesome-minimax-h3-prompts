# Fantasy, Sci-fi and VFX Video Prompts

Original MiniMax H3 prompts for controlled transformations, speculative worlds, practical visual-effects logic, and original genre imagery.

## VFX-001 Glasshouse Grows a Night Sky

**Status:** Concept — not independently tested

**Use it for:** A poetic VFX film where a familiar room transforms through one consistent physical rule.

**Mode:** Glasshouse image + plant references + optional sound design reference  
**Format:** 16:9, target 12 seconds  
**Reference map:** Image 1 = exact architecture; Images 2–3 = verified plant shapes; Audio 1 = pacing only.

```text
Create an original magical-realist transformation inside the glasshouse from Image 1. Preserve every beam, pane, table, and doorway. Use the fern and white night-blooming flower shapes from Images 2–3 without adding new species.

At dusk, one white flower opens on the center table. Its pollen rises as tiny points of cool light; each point reflects correctly in nearby glass. The lights drift upward and settle against the roof panes, gradually becoming a miniature star field contained only inside the glasshouse. Fern shadows stretch across the floor as if lit by this new sky. Camera makes one slow orbit around the table and ends facing the unchanged doorway, where the real outdoor sky remains cloudy and ordinary.

Sound intent: glass ticks, leaves, low airy resonance, no voice. Keep the transformation gentle and spatially bounded. Avoid galaxy portals, exploding plants, altered architecture, extra flowers, copied constellation shapes, unreadable magical text, people, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Architecture lock, reflection logic, plant identity, effect boundary, and understated progression.

## VFX-002 Miniature Rain Collector City

**Status:** Concept — not independently tested

**Use it for:** A sustainable-world concept film showing an understandable system at miniature scale.

**Mode:** City model image + mechanism diagram + rain audio  
**Format:** 16:9, target 12 seconds  
**Reference map:** Image 1 = miniature city style; Image 2 = verified water path; Audio 1 = rainfall texture only.

```text
Generate a tactile tabletop miniature city during a rain test. Preserve the six-block layout, pale wood buildings, blue ceramic channels, and central glass reservoir from Image 1. Follow the water path in Image 2 exactly: roofs → gutters → street channels → filter garden → reservoir.

Opening macro: controlled rain begins over one block; droplets collect on sloped roofs and enter tiny gutters. Camera follows one stream at miniature height as it joins the street channel and moves downhill. Middle overhead: water passes through the planted filter garden; the flow becomes visibly clearer without an instant magical change. Final wide: the central reservoir level rises slightly while a small mechanical indicator moves from low to medium.

Use real surface tension, scale-appropriate droplets, wood darkening, and ceramic reflections. Sound intent: close rain, small water trickle, mechanical click. Avoid real skyscraper scale, floods, invented labels or efficiency claims, reverse water flow, extra buildings, people, logos, or watermark.
```

**Review:** System mapping, scale cues, water direction, reservoir level, and no unsupported sustainability claim.

## VFX-003 Constellation Dress Performance

**Status:** Concept — not independently tested

**Use it for:** A high-fashion fantasy piece with precise material transformation and performer continuity.

**Mode:** Adult model reference + dress image + movement video + light motif image  
**Format:** 9:16, target 10 seconds  
**Reference map:** Image 1 = fictional adult model; Image 2 = exact dress; Video 1 = arm movement; Image 3 = point-light pattern only.

```text
Create a vertical performance in a dark matte studio. Preserve the adult model from Image 1 and the full-length black silk dress from Image 2: asymmetric neckline, fitted waist, wide lower skirt, no jewelry. Use Video 1 only for the slow arm path and Image 3 only for the density of small point lights.

The model begins still in side profile. As one arm rises, tiny silver points appear along existing dress seams, never on skin. The points connect through fine luminous threads that follow the fabric folds; the skirt remains real silk with weight and shadow. At the top of the arm movement, camera arcs to the front and the lights lift a few centimeters from the dress, forming an abstract canopy while the garment itself remains unchanged. The model lowers the hand and the lights settle back into the seams.

Sound intent: silk movement, low harmonic pulse, no vocals. Avoid real constellation copies, nudity, face drift, floating dress, body reshaping, particle explosion, text, logos, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Model identity, dress construction, particle attachment, fabric weight, and reversible transformation.
