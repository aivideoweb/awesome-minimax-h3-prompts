# Food and Beverage Video Prompts

Original MiniMax H3 prompts for preparation, texture, service, restaurant storytelling, and physically credible food motion.

## FNB-001 Dawn Bakery Lamination Sequence

**Use it for:** A craft-focused bakery film showing process rather than generic beauty shots.

**Mode:** Kitchen image + baker identity + dough reference  
**Format:** 16:9, target 12 seconds  
**Reference map:** Image 1 = kitchen layout; Image 2 = fictional adult baker identity and uniform; Image 3 = dough thickness and fold structure.

```text
Create a dawn craft film inside the small bakery shown in Image 1. Preserve the adult baker from Image 2: same face, short hair, navy apron, cream shirt. Use Image 3 only for the laminated dough structure.

0–4s: macro side view as the rolling pin compresses chilled dough evenly; flour dust moves lightly and settles, hands remain anatomically correct. 4–8s: overhead view as the baker completes one precise letter fold, aligns the edges, and presses the seam once; tools and dough do not teleport between cuts. 8–12s: medium shot at the oven as a finished tray is removed and set on a rack; warm steam and butter sheen appear naturally, while the baker exhales and relaxes their shoulders.

Natural mixed pre-dawn blue window light and warm oven light. Sound intent: rolling wood, dough fold, tray slide, oven fan, no music. Avoid instant transformation from raw dough, excessive flour clouds, unsafe bare-hand oven contact, extra fingers, branded packaging, text, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Hand technique, process continuity, food safety, dough physics, and tool persistence.

## FNB-002 Clear Broth Noodle Service

**Use it for:** A restaurant hero clip where liquid, noodles, and garnish remain controlled.

**Mode:** Dish image + counter image + optional motion reference  
**Format:** 4:5, target 8–10 seconds  
**Reference map:** Image 1 = exact bowl and final dish; Image 2 = serving counter; Video 1 = ladle movement only.

```text
Generate a close, appetizing service sequence for a fictional noodle bar. Preserve the speckled gray bowl, pale clear broth, thin noodles, two mushroom slices, green onion, and half marinated egg from Image 1. Use the dark oak counter and brushed steel service area from Image 2.

Start with the empty bowl centered. One cook's hand lowers a neat coil of noodles with chopsticks; the noodles retain length and weight. A ladle pours clear broth in one continuous stream, raising the liquid level without overflowing. The same hand places exactly two mushrooms and one egg half, then finishes with a small pinch of green onion. Camera makes a subtle forward slide and stops as steam rises against side light.

Sound intent: broth pour, ceramic contact, quiet kitchen ambience. Avoid extra toppings, multiplying noodles, milky broth, floating utensils, hand-food fusion, impossible steam, fake restaurant text, excessive oil shine, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Ingredient count, liquid level, hand continuity, garnish placement, and realistic steam.

## FNB-003 Sparkling Botanical Tea Macro Ad

**Use it for:** A beverage ad built around bubbles, glass, and an original flavor identity.

**Mode:** Bottle image + ingredient reference image  
**Format:** 9:16, target 8 seconds  
**Reference map:** Image 1 = exact fictional bottle; Image 2 = rosemary and white peach appearance only.

```text
Create a vertical macro beverage film for the fictional “MEADOW FIZZ” white-peach rosemary tea. Preserve the clear fluted bottle, pale gold liquid, cream label, and sage cap from Image 1; label text must remain unchanged and no claims may be added.

Opening macro: cold bottle rests on limestone, tiny carbonation trails rise through the liquid while one condensation bead forms at the shoulder. Middle: cap opens with a restrained release, then the drink pours into a tall clear glass; bubbles gather at the real rosemary sprig and peach slice shown in Image 2. Final: camera arcs slightly around the glass as the liquid settles, keeping the bottle label visible behind it and an empty upper safe area.

Use bright diffused daylight, realistic refraction, clean highlights, and pale natural color. Sound intent: cap release, precise pour, soft fizz, one leaf movement. Avoid fruit explosions, floating ingredients, excessive foam, changing bottle ridges, mirrored label, extra slogans, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Bottle fidelity, carbonation scale, glass refraction, label legibility, and ingredient realism.
