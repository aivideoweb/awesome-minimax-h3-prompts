# Cinematic Storytelling Video Prompts

Original MiniMax H3 prompts for compact narrative arcs, character performance, relationship tension, mystery, and emotional continuity.

## CIN-001 The Unsent Library Letter

**Use it for:** A quiet relationship scene told through objects, eye lines, and one decision.

**Mode:** Two character references + location image + optional room-tone audio  
**Format:** 16:9, target 15 seconds  
**Reference map:** Images 1–2 = fictional adult character identities and wardrobe; Image 3 = library layout; Audio 1 = ambience only.

```text
Create a restrained contemporary drama in the old reading room shown in Image 3. Preserve Character A from Image 1: adult woman, cropped dark hair, brown cardigan, silver ring. Preserve Character B from Image 2: adult man, wavy hair, navy coat, canvas satchel. Keep both identities and wardrobe unchanged.

0–5s: medium close-up on A at a long table; she finishes a handwritten letter, folds it once, and hears the distant door. 5–10s: over A's shoulder, B enters at the far end and pauses without seeing her; A covers the letter with a library book, her hand hesitating on the cover. 10–15s: close two-plane composition—B turns toward the aisle, A looks up, their eyes almost align, then A quietly slides the unopened letter into the book and stands.

Use winter window light, quiet dust, minimal camera drift, natural breathing and micro-expressions. Sound intent: pen scratch, page fold, door hinge, radiator tick; no generated dialogue. Avoid melodramatic crying, age drift, hand errors, changing letter position, extra patrons, readable private letter text, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Eye-line logic, letter continuity, subtle performance, identity locks, and action clarity without dialogue.

## CIN-002 Last Train Platform Farewell

**Use it for:** A compact emotional scene with strong screen direction and environmental timing.

**Mode:** Character references + station image + train motion reference  
**Format:** 2.39:1, target 12–15 seconds  
**Reference map:** Images 1–2 = fictional adult characters; Image 3 = station architecture; Video 1 = train speed only.

```text
Generate a late-night farewell at the small outdoor station in Image 3. Character A from Image 1 stays screen-left in a dark green raincoat; Character B from Image 2 stays screen-right inside the train doorway wearing a gray scarf. Maintain the 180-degree line, face identity, wet weather, and positions across cuts.

Opening wide: rain streaks under platform lights while the train waits; A approaches but stops two meters from the door. Middle alternating close-ups: B grips the door rail, A opens a hand as if to pass a small paper ticket but decides not to; both hold eye contact with restrained emotion. Final side wide: warning light flashes, the door closes between them, the train moves rightward at the pace of Video 1, and A remains still as reflected carriage light crosses the raincoat.

Sound intent: rain, electrical hum, door warning, rail movement, no dialogue or music. Avoid swapping screen sides, changing scarf or coat, train passing through the platform, exaggerated lip movement, readable operator logos, unsafe track access, or watermark.
```

**Review:** 180-degree continuity, train/door physics, reflective light, prop persistence, and emotional restraint.

## CIN-003 Rooftop Weather Signal Mystery

**Use it for:** A mystery teaser driven by practical clues rather than exposition.

**Mode:** Character image + rooftop location + prop image + optional sound motif  
**Format:** 9:16, target 12 seconds  
**Reference map:** Image 1 = fictional adult technician; Image 2 = rooftop geometry; Image 3 = hand-built receiver; Audio 1 = short three-note signal only.

```text
Create a vertical mystery teaser on the apartment rooftop shown in Image 2 during an approaching storm. Preserve the adult technician from Image 1: shaved hair, orange waterproof jacket, black gloves. Preserve the receiver from Image 3: compact metal box, fold-out antenna, analog needle, one green indicator.

Opening: technician kneels beside the receiver while distant clouds pulse with silent sheet lightning; camera slowly rises from the green indicator to the face. Middle: the antenna rotates by itself exactly twenty degrees, the analog needle moves in three measured steps synchronized to Audio 1, and the technician removes one glove to feel a faint vibration on the metal roof. Ending: technician looks toward a dark neighboring building; one window answers with the same three green pulses, then all rooftop power cuts out and the frame holds in natural darkness.

Sound intent: wind, antenna servo, three-note signal, sudden electrical silence. Avoid visible aliens, magical beams, unsafe lightning strike, changing receiver design, extra windows flashing, text overlays, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Clue order, device continuity, synchronized pulses, low-light visibility, and controlled reveal.
