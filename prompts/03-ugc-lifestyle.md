# UGC and Lifestyle Video Prompts

Original MiniMax H3 prompts for creator-style demonstrations, everyday routines, credible social proof, and natural handheld footage.

## UGC-001 Desk Lamp Honest First Impression

**Status:** Concept — not independently tested

**Use it for:** A creator-style product reaction that feels specific without fabricating a testimonial.

**Mode:** Creator reference + product image + optional room-tone audio  
**Format:** 9:16, target 12 seconds  
**Reference map:** Image 1 = fictional adult creator identity; Image 2 = exact lamp; Audio 1 = room ambience only.

```text
Make a natural vertical first-impression clip in a small home office at dusk. Preserve the adult creator from Image 1 and the “FOLDLINE” desk lamp from Image 2: thin black stem, oval base, warm-white light bar, single brass hinge. This is a fictional demonstration, not a real customer endorsement.

Opening: handheld phone camera is already rolling as the creator places the lamp beside a notebook and says no generated dialogue; expression is curious, not exaggerated. Middle: one hand unfolds the hinge, taps the base, and slides a sketch under the light; exposure adjusts once and settles while the creator leans closer to inspect the page. Ending: creator looks toward the camera, gives a small approving nod, then rotates the light away to show reduced screen glare.

Use believable phone focus, mild micro-shake, ordinary room texture, and correct hand-object contact. Sound intent: hinge friction, base tap, paper slide, quiet street outside. Avoid sales captions, five-star claims, over-smoothed skin, impossible lamp articulation, hand fusion, gaze drift, sponsored-brand overlays, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Honest framing, creator identity, lamp mechanics, phone-camera realism, and claim safety.

## UGC-002 Balcony Herb Kit Weekend Routine

**Status:** Concept — not independently tested

**Use it for:** A calm lifestyle montage showing a simple process and realistic progress.

**Mode:** Location image + kit image + optional action reference video  
**Format:** 9:16, target 15 seconds  
**Reference map:** Image 1 = balcony layout; Image 2 = kit components; Video 1 = hand pace only, not identity.

```text
Create a three-beat weekend routine around the fictional “WINDOW PATCH” herb starter kit. Preserve the narrow apartment balcony from Image 1 and the exact three terracotta pots, seed envelopes, wood markers, and shallow watering cup from Image 2.

Beat 1: over-the-shoulder phone shot as an adult fills the three pots with soil; loose soil falls with real weight and stays where it lands. Beat 2: macro view of fingers placing three different seeds, then writing only simple blank marker strokes—no readable generated plant names. Beat 3: later that morning, the person waters each pot once, wipes one drop from the railing, and sits back with tea; do not fake instant plant growth.

Natural cloudy daylight, apartment sounds, occasional breeze, modest handheld movement. Preserve pot count and arrangement. Sound intent: soil, paper packet, water, distant traffic, one bird. Avoid time-lapse growth, spotless staged surfaces, extra gardening products, unsafe leaning over railing, text, third-party logos, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Process order, object count, soil/water physics, authenticity, and absence of false results.

## UGC-003 Commuter Sling Real-World Packing Test

**Status:** Concept — not independently tested

**Use it for:** Demonstrating capacity and access in a believable daily commute.

**Mode:** Bag images + creator identity + item layout image  
**Format:** 4:5, target 10–12 seconds  
**Reference map:** Images 1–2 = bag exterior/interior; Image 3 = fictional creator; Image 4 = exact everyday items.

```text
Create a practical packing test for the fictional “CROSSDAY” commuter sling. Reconstruct one bag from Images 1–2: slate fabric, curved zipper, padded olive interior, one front pocket, one adjustable strap. Preserve the adult creator from Image 3 and use only the items shown in Image 4: small camera, wallet, keys, earbuds case, folded transit map.

Top-down opening: creator places each item beside the empty bag, then packs them one at a time without objects shrinking or clipping. Side medium shot: zipper closes with mild resistance and the bag retains realistic volume. Final walking shot: creator wears it across the chest, reaches the front pocket for the transit map while stopping safely beside a station wall; camera tracks two steps and settles.

Natural morning light and real fabric compression. Sound intent: zipper, fabric, keys, station ambience. Avoid impossible capacity, vanishing items, extra pockets, floating strap, unsafe walking into traffic, fake durability claims, other logos, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Bag construction, item persistence, realistic capacity, strap behavior, and safe action.
