## MUS-001 Rooftop Trio Live Session

**Use it for:** A believable one-take performance film with synchronized playing, camera rhythm, and environmental sound.

**Mode:** Cast images + location image + rights-cleared music reference  \
**Format:** 16:9, target 12–15 seconds  \
**Reference map:** Images 1–3 = performer identity, wardrobe, and instrument; Image 4 = rooftop geometry; Audio 1 = tempo, section boundary, and performance energy only.

```text
Create a restrained live-session film for the fictional trio “AFTER RAIN”. Preserve the three adult performers, wardrobe, instrument shapes, and left-to-right stage positions from Images 1–3. Rebuild the small rooftop from Image 4 without adding skyline landmarks or visible brands.

Begin on the drummer's brush circling the snare. At the first chord, glide backward to reveal upright bass on screen left and electric piano on screen right. Keep hand movement causally matched to the heard rhythm: brush stroke, bass pluck, then keyboard chord. During the final musical phrase, make a slow 30-degree arc while sunset reflections move naturally across the instruments; end on the performers sharing a quiet smile without stopping the phrase.

Sound intent: preserve the timing and dynamic contour of Audio 1, add only subtle rooftop wind and distant city ambience, no crowd or invented lyrics. Keep instrument contact, finger count, cable placement, tempo, and screen direction continuous. Avoid lip movement, impossible playing technique, duplicated limbs, changing instruments, famous-person resemblance, copied album imagery, logos, captions, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Beat synchronization, playable hand positions, performer identity, instrument geometry, audio rights, and clean ending.

## MUS-002 Multilingual Station Duet

**Use it for:** A short dramatic duet in two languages with clear speaker ownership and natural turn-taking.

**Mode:** Two cast references + location reference + optional voice references with consent  \
**Format:** 2.39:1, target 12 seconds  \
**Reference map:** Images 1–2 = adult performer identity and wardrobe; Image 3 = fictional station platform; Audios 1–2 = each performer's authorized voice identity only.

```text
Create an intimate, fictional musical exchange on an almost-empty covered station platform at blue hour. Preserve Performer A from Image 1 in a charcoal coat on screen left and Performer B from Image 2 in a rust scarf on screen right. Preserve the platform roof and bench geometry from Image 3; no real rail operator marks.

0–4 seconds: medium two-shot, distant train hum. Performer A sings one soft Mandarin line, “下一站，我们还会再见,” with a calm, hopeful expression. Performer B listens without lip movement. 4–8 seconds: cut on a station-light flicker to Performer B, who answers in Spanish, “La próxima vez, llegaré temprano.” Performer A now listens. 8–12 seconds: return to the two-shot as they sing a wordless two-note harmony; a train light passes behind them and the camera gently pushes in.

Keep exact voice-to-face ownership, natural breathing, intelligible pronunciation, stable eyelines, consistent platform geography, and modest performance. Sound intent: voices foreground, light rail ambience, no additional lyrics, no non-diegetic music. Avoid translated subtitles baked into the image, overlapping mouth movement, voice swapping, melodramatic gestures, real transit logos, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Language pronunciation, speaker attribution, lip synchronization, identity, eyelines, and voice consent.

## MUS-004 Sound-to-Shape Album Loop

**Use it for:** An abstract looping visualizer built from a rights-cleared audio motif without copying album art.

**Mode:** Text + audio reference + optional palette image  \
**Format:** 1:1, seamless 8-second loop  \
**Reference map:** Audio 1 = onset timing, intensity, and frequency bands; Image 1 = color relationships only, not composition.

```text
Build an original square audio-reactive loop for a fictional instrumental release. A matte ceramic ring floats above black sand in a dark studio. Map Audio 1 deliberately: low pulses lift concentric ripples in the sand, mid-frequency notes travel as soft amber light around the ring, and the single high accent releases a brief cloud of pale blue particles that returns to the ring.

Camera remains on a slow clockwise orbital path and returns to its exact starting position. The ceramic ring must remain one continuous object with constant thickness and no logo. At second 0 the sand is flat except for one shallow ripple; by second 7 the particles settle and the ripple reverses smoothly into the identical opening state. Use restrained physical motion, realistic shadows, and generous center space for typography added later in editing.

Sound intent: Audio 1 remains unchanged; generate no speech or extra music. Avoid generic equalizer bars, readable text, copied cover layouts, kaleidoscope overload, strobing, sudden camera jumps, broken loop continuity, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Audio-event mapping, loop seam, object topology, flash safety, originality, and typography space.
