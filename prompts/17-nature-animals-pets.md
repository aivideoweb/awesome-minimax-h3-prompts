# Nature, Animals and Pet Video Prompts

Original MiniMax H3 prompts for ethical wildlife observation, pet content, plant studies, and macro nature films. Never stage distress, disturb wildlife, or imply veterinary conclusions from generated media.

## NAT-001 Urban Fox Dawn Observation

**Use it for:** A documentary-style wildlife moment with restrained camera behavior and no baiting narrative.

**Mode:** Rights-cleared habitat footage + species reference images + ambient audio  \
**Format:** 16:9, target 12 seconds  \
**Reference map:** Video 1 = location, light, and camera position; Images 1–3 = adult red fox anatomy and coat variation; Audio 1 = habitat ambience only.

```text
Create a quiet dawn observation of one adult red fox crossing the edge of a fictional urban allotment. Use Video 1 for hedge placement, wet path, fence, and natural light. Preserve plausible fox proportions, gait, coat, ears, tail weight, and cautious behavior from Images 1–3 without copying a distinctive photographed individual.

The camera stays hidden at low telephoto height and never approaches. The fox emerges from the hedge, pauses to listen with one ear turning, walks across the wet path, briefly sniffs beside an empty planter, then exits through a second hedge opening. Keep the route continuous and the animal at a respectful distance. A cyclist passes softly out of focus in the far background after the fox has cleared the path.

Sound intent: early birds, distant bicycle, leaves, no dramatic music or growls. Avoid eye glow, pet-like posing, feeding, den disclosure, pursuit, cubs, aggression, fantasy behavior, species mixing, generated location claims, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Species anatomy, gait, ethical distance, habitat continuity, behavior, and absence of sensationalism.

## NAT-002 Senior Dog Raincoat Fit Check

**Use it for:** A warm pet-care social clip that demonstrates garment fit without making health claims.

**Mode:** Consented pet images + garment images + owner hands reference  \
**Format:** 9:16, target 10 seconds  \
**Reference map:** Images 1–3 = exact dog identity and markings; Images 4–5 = raincoat construction; Image 6 = authorized adult owner's hands only.

```text
Create a gentle vertical fit-check for one senior mixed-breed dog in a quiet entryway. Preserve the dog's face, coat markings, body size, gray muzzle, and calm movement from Images 1–3. Preserve the mustard raincoat's two straps, reflective piping, hood shape, and opening from Images 4–5.

Begin with the dog standing naturally on a nonslip mat. The adult owner fastens the chest strap, slides two fingers beneath it to show comfortable space, and checks that the coat does not restrict the front legs. Cut to a side view as the dog takes four unhurried steps toward the door, pauses, and looks back. End with the hood still down and all paws visible.

Sound intent: fabric fastening, light paw steps, home ambience, no speech. Do not claim medical, orthopedic, waterproof, or calming benefits. Avoid forcing posture, exaggerated excitement, changing markings, extra straps, tight fit, covered eyes, distorted paws, child handler, logos, captions, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Pet identity, comfort signals, garment geometry, paw anatomy, claim safety, and owner consent.

## NAT-003 Balcony Tomato Growth Diary

**Use it for:** A compact seasonal growth story with consistent plant structure and honest time compression.

**Mode:** Same-plant weekly images + balcony reference + optional date record  \
**Format:** 4:5, target 12 seconds  \
**Reference map:** Images 1–6 = one documented plant at successive stages; Image 7 = fixed balcony layout; Text 1 = verified capture order only.

```text
Create a clearly time-compressed growth diary for one balcony tomato plant. Preserve the pot, stake, balcony rail, wall marks, and camera angle from Image 7. Follow Images 1–6 in chronological order: small transplant, new leaves, first flowers, small green fruit, larger green fruit, then one ripe red fruit. Do not invent additional yield.

Use a locked camera and gentle dissolves anchored on the main stem and blue tie. Between stages, sunlight and weather may change naturally, but the plant must retain branching continuity and the same minor leaf blemish. At the final stage, an adult hand enters once, turns the single ripe tomato without picking it, and exits. Hold a clean final frame for an editorial date added later.

Sound intent: evolving balcony ambience from spring breeze to summer insects, no narration. Avoid instant magical growth, multiplying fruit, changing pot or rail, impossible leaf motion, pesticide claims, generated dates, brand packaging, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Chronology, stem topology, fruit count, environmental continuity, honest time compression, and date accuracy.

## NAT-004 Tide-Pool Macro Field Note

**Use it for:** An educational macro sequence showing small marine life without handling or relocating it.

**Mode:** Rights-cleared macro references + habitat image + verified species note  \
**Format:** 16:9, target 10–12 seconds  \
**Reference map:** Images 1–3 = anatomy and color range of the selected common anemone; Image 4 = exact tide-pool arrangement; Text 1 = verified behavior boundaries.

```text
Create a patient macro field note at a fictional rocky tide pool during a naturally low tide. Preserve the pool shape, pebble positions, wet rock texture, and one common sea anemone from the references. The camera is mounted above the water and never touches the animal.

Open on a wide macro context with gentle surface ripples. Slowly push closer as the anemone's tentacles move with the water rather than reaching toward the camera. One tiny unidentifiable crustacean crosses the background once; do not make it the subject or imply predation. A small wave refreshes the pool, briefly distorting the view, then clears to the same composition. End with open space for a verified species label added in post.

Sound intent: close water movement and distant shore, no music. Avoid handling, exposed dry animal, dramatic hunting, fluorescent fantasy color, extra species, impossible anatomy, generated scientific text, exact-location disclosure, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Animal anatomy, water-driven motion, non-interference, species claim, location privacy, and label space.
