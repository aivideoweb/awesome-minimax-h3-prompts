# Fashion and Beauty Video Prompts

Original MiniMax H3 prompts for editorial motion, product-focused beauty films, wardrobe continuity, and tasteful transformations.

## FSH-001 Wind-Study Eyewear Editorial

**Use it for:** A controlled fashion film that keeps eyewear geometry and model identity stable.

**Mode:** Model reference + eyewear multi-view images + motion reference  
**Format:** 9:16, target 10 seconds  
**Reference map:** Image 1 = fictional adult model identity; Images 2–3 = eyewear geometry; Video 1 = head-turn tempo only.

```text
Create a minimal vertical eyewear editorial in a warm-gray seamless studio. Preserve the adult model from Image 1: same face, short textured hair, natural skin, charcoal high-neck top. Reconstruct the exact eyewear from Images 2–3: translucent smoke frame, narrow oval lenses, silver hinge, straight temples.

Opening close-up: model faces three-quarters away while a controlled fan moves only a few hair strands and the collar edge. Camera performs a slow lateral slide as the model turns toward lens at the tempo of Video 1. Midway, light sweeps once across the lenses without hiding the eyes. End on a steady frontal close-up; model lowers the chin slightly and holds a calm gaze.

Use one large soft key, thin rim, natural pores, and honest lens reflections. No beauty-copy text. Avoid changing frame width, lens tint, hinge location, face shape, eye color, duplicated glasses, dramatic hair storm, plastic skin, unrelated jewelry, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Face identity, eyewear geometry, lens reflections, subtle performance, and clean vertical crop.

## FSH-002 Mineral Lip Color Texture Study

**Use it for:** A beauty macro that emphasizes truthful material and restrained application.

**Mode:** Product image + texture swatch + adult model reference  
**Format:** 4:5, target 8 seconds  
**Reference map:** Image 1 = exact case; Image 2 = approved terracotta-rose shade; Image 3 = fictional adult model identity.

```text
Produce a tactile beauty film for a fictional mineral lip color. Preserve the brushed aluminum rectangular case and small circular terracotta insert from Image 1. Match only the approved color and satin finish from Image 2. Preserve the adult model from Image 3 without changing lip shape, skin tone, or facial features.

Start with extreme macro as the cap slides open on its real rail. Cut to a clean applicator picking up a small amount of product; texture remains creamy, not liquid. In a side close-up, the model applies one controlled stroke to the lower lip and presses lips together once. End on the closed case beside a single honest color swatch, with the model softly out of focus behind.

Neutral daylight, true skin texture, accurate color management. Sound intent: metal slide, applicator touch, room tone. Avoid lip enlargement, skin whitening, color drift, glitter, dripping product, extra swatches, medical claims, brand imitation, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Shade consistency, model identity, application anatomy, product mechanism, and no retouching distortion.

## FSH-003 Four-Look Textile Transition

**Use it for:** A fashion transformation where the transition is driven by material, not a copied social effect.

**Mode:** Model reference + four outfit references + simple movement video  
**Format:** 9:16, target 12 seconds  
**Reference map:** Image 1 = fixed adult model; Images 2–5 = outfits A–D; Video 1 = continuous turn path only.

```text
Create an original four-look transition in a neutral daylight studio. Preserve the adult model from Image 1—same face, body proportions, hair, and understated makeup. Each outfit must match its own reference: A cream linen set, B deep green pleated dress, C rust wool coat with straight trousers, D midnight-blue sculptural suit. Never blend garments.

The model performs one slow clockwise turn. At four natural occlusion moments, a real textile panel passes close to the lens: sheer linen reveals Look A; pleated fabric reveals B; wool texture reveals C; smooth satin reveals D. The camera remains fixed at waist-to-head framing; model position and scale do not shift. Garments respond to the same turn momentum and settle before the next transition. Finish with Look D and a steady direct gaze.

Sound intent: four distinct fabric movements, soft foot pivot, no music required. Avoid pop-in cuts, layered outfits, identity drift, body reshaping, background changes, copied interface stickers, text, logos, or watermark.
```

**Review:** Exact outfit separation, occlusion timing, body continuity, fabric behavior, and original transition language.
