# Fictional canal scene — single-image starter

[English](#english) · [简体中文](#简体中文)

## English

**Status:** Concept — not independently tested

This is a separate practice adaptation for the supplied fictional image, not a new canonical recipe or a verified result. It does not increase the 84-recipe count.

![Fictional canal neighborhood after rain](../assets/gallery/rain-washed-canal-morning.webp)

**Input:** Use the image above as one first-frame reference in a tool that explicitly supports a single starting image. Do not infer that every H3 interface supports this mode. VideoWeb’s free-tool guide describes a start/end image pair; this single-image exercise is not a ready-to-upload pair. For that tool, use the [text-only starter](./videoweb-quick-start.md) instead.

**Target:** 5 seconds, 16:9; choose compatible settings in your tool.

```text
Animate the supplied image into a five-second single continuous shot of a fictional canal neighborhood after rain. Use the image as the exact starting composition. Keep the buildings, rooflines, bridge shape, canal width, bicycle position and existing people unchanged. Hold the camera still. Let only small ripples move across the water and a few droplets fall from the roof edges; existing people remain still. End on the same quiet composition. Preserve soft overcast light, wet stone and muted green reflections. No cuts, new people, new boats, new buildings, readable signs or landmarks. Do not imply this is footage of a real destination. Sound intent, if supported: gentle water and occasional drips, no speech or music.
```

**Review:** Check bridge and building shapes, stable people and bicycle, small water motion and the absence of invented landmarks. This is a fictional scene, not destination evidence.

The original [TRV-001](../prompts/04-travel-hospitality.md#trv-001-rain-washed-canal-town-morning) remains a real-location workflow requiring three verified reference images. The single generated image above cannot satisfy those requirements.

## 简体中文

**状态：** 概念稿，未经独立生成验证。

这是为现有虚构图片补充的练习版本，不是新增的正式配方或已验证结果，不计入 84 条配方。

**输入：** 将上图作为唯一首帧参考，使用明确支持单张首帧的工具。不同 H3 界面支持的输入可能不同。VideoWeb 免费工具的现有指南要求首尾两张图；本练习不是完整图片对。在该工具中可先使用[纯文字入门示例](./videoweb-quick-start.md)。

**目标：** 5 秒、16:9；在工具中选择支持的设置。

```text
将提供的图片制作成 5 秒连续单镜头，场景是雨后的虚构水乡街区。严格以上传图片作为起始构图，保持建筑、屋顶轮廓、桥形、河道宽度、自行车位置和现有人物不变。镜头固定。只让水面出现轻微涟漪，屋檐偶尔滴水；现有人物保持静止。以同样安静的构图结束。保持柔和阴天光、湿润石材与灰绿色倒影。不切镜头，不新增人物、船只、建筑、可读招牌或地标。不暗示这是真实旅游目的地的拍摄画面。若工具支持声音，使用轻柔水声与偶尔滴水声，不要对白或音乐。
```

**检查：** 桥梁与建筑形状是否稳定，人物和自行车是否移动或变形，水面动作是否克制，是否出现虚构地标。不能将生成结果当作真实地点的证据。

原版 [TRV-001](../prompts/04-travel-hospitality.md#trv-001-rain-washed-canal-town-morning)仍要求三张经过核实的地点参考图。这一张生成图片不能替代那些输入。
