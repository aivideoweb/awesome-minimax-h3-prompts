# Originality and Provenance Policy

The canonical `prompts/` library inherits the independently authored Flaq AI cookbook under MIT; see [UPSTREAM.md](../UPSTREAM.md). The independent-authorship rules below apply to new contributions. A separate attributed X showcase provides external examples for study without classifying them as original recipes.

## Attributed community showcase

The [X community showcase](./x-community-showcase.md) may include creator handles, original-post links, remotely referenced thumbnails and videos, short prompt quotations, and clearly labeled editorial analysis. This exception applies only to attributed showcases and their README previews, not to original recipe submissions.

Record the post ID, author, prompt location, original media URLs, retrieval method, check date and observed limitations in [the source manifest](./x-community-sources.json). Preserve attribution and distinguish direct retrieval, third-party-reader retrieval, sampled visual review and independent regeneration. Missing reference assets or visible output mismatches must be stated. Link full creator prompts at their source; do not infer redistribution rights from public access.

External prompts and media remain subject to their owners' rights and are not covered by this repository's MIT license. Community entries do not increase original prompt or asset counts. Rights concerns can be reported through a repository issue identifying the affected source.

## What may be researched

Common video-production needs may be studied at the scenario level: product launches, creator demos, travel films, food preparation, fashion motion, narrative scenes, sports, animation, VFX, interfaces, match cuts, and visual comedy. These are general fields of creative work rather than repository-specific expression.

## What must be independently authored

Every committed recipe must create its own:

- Title, description, prompt wording, field structure, and review notes;
- Fictional people, brands, products, places, interfaces, props, and dialogue;
- Story premise, action order, camera path, timing, sound design, and ending;
- Multimodal reference roles, conflict priorities, continuity locks, and edit scope;
- Visual brief, generated image, filename, alt text, and production record.

Changing a model name, synonym, language, character name, or a few adjectives is not independent authorship.

## Content excluded from the original recipe library

- External author names, handles, biographies, profile links, or contributor branding;
- Gallery calls to action, tracking links, star charts, promotional widgets, or copied statistics;
- Third-party thumbnails, videos, screenshots, watermarks, captions, or campaign assets;
- Prompt text copied, translated, lightly paraphrased, reordered, or mechanically expanded from another source;
- Protected characters, celebrity likenesses, living-artist imitation, real testimonials, or recognizable campaign concepts;
- Model capability claims without a direct primary-source link.

## Upstream creation workflow (historical record)

1. Audit broad deliverable coverage and identify practical gaps;
2. Define a repository-specific taxonomy around commercial production risks;
3. Create fictional products, characters, locations, interfaces, and narratives from a blank page;
4. Write each recipe using the H3 reference-map and control-contract framework;
5. Remove real brands, known characters, identifiable creators, and unsupported claims;
6. Generate new visual assets from repository-owned image briefs;
7. Record the actual generation tool and post-processing without false model attribution;
8. Check the original recipe library for imported author branding, and check repository links, counts, IDs, and media records; separately verify attribution in community showcases.

## Contribution review

Reviewers should ask:

- Does a search for a distinctive 8–12 word phrase reveal a likely source?
- Are the story, camera, transitions, dialogue, and fictional product clearly original?
- Does the contribution contain unexplained author metadata or a pre-existing watermark?
- Are media rights, consent, model/tool, inputs, settings, and edits documented?
- Are factual and model claims connected to primary sources?

When provenance is uncertain, maintainers may request drafts, generation records, source files, or removal of the contribution. Good-faith rights concerns should be raised through a GitHub issue without publishing private personal information.
