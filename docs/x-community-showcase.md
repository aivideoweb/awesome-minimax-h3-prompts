## Reading the evidence

- Original X pages returned HTTP 403 to the research client. Public post text and associated media were retrieved through the third-party FxTwitter reader; retrieval URLs are recorded in the manifest.
- Upstream entries XH3-001–006 retain their September 20 three-second sampling records. New entries XH3-007–015 were decoded from a published MP4 and sampled about every four seconds. Notes describe sampled frames, not full playback or audio-quality review.
- Model identity and generation setup are author statements. We did not rerun the prompts or authenticate the generation backend. Uploaded file duration/resolution may reflect editing or platform transcoding.
- Thumbnails link to the original X video player, with direct MP4 and original-post fallbacks. No third-party media files are committed; remote URLs may change.
- Authors retain rights to their videos, images and prompt text. These items are not relicensed under this repository's MIT license.
