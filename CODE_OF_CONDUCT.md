# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and respectful;
- Critique the contribution, not the contributor;
- Acknowledge sources, rights, consent, and uncertainty;
- Accept good-faith review and correction;
- Protect private information and report sensitive concerns discreetly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, or personal attacks;
- Publishing private information without permission;
- Deliberate plagiarism, impersonation, deceptive media, or misrepresentation of model results;
- Repeated disruption of project discussions or reviews.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, hide, or remove contributions and participation that violate these standards. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through an appropriate GitHub contact channel. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports in good faith and protect reporter privacy where practical.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.
